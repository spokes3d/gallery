Spokes are ionization zones that rotate along the racetrack of a magnetron, mostly seen in
high-power impulse magnetron sputtering (HiPIMS). SPOKES3D, a Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowship
at LPGP (CNRS / Université Paris-Saclay), simulates them in 3D with particle-in-cell codes.

This gallery holds the project's public studies: the verification of the codes, demonstrations
of the methods and examples. Each study is a complete, standalone script with its result
cached, and its status box says what it verifies and what it does not. Studies with new results
join the gallery when their preprint is out.
